MODE NOTES
----------
OFF:
  - Same DPDK datapath/test pipeline.
  - GreenQUIC policy bypassed.
  - Tuning variables do not drive the GreenQUIC policy.

BASIC:
  - Uses physical DPDK policy signals.
  - Physical knobs such as RX_QUEUE_HIGH and PRESSURE_UP apply.

PLUS:
  - Uses the same physical policy plus QUIC semantic hints.
  - ACTIVE_TRANSFER_SLEEP_MIN_LEVEL is PLUS-only.
